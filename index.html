Why online trade needs to change

Buying online shouldn’t be a leap of faith.

Buyers fear paying for something that never arrives. Sellers fear delivering something they never get paid for. Traditional escrow can help, but it introduces another party to trust, extra steps, fees, and delays.

A better way to transact

Neither side should have to trust the other with everything.

Zecrow is designed around shared control. Instead of allowing one participant to authorize an escrow payout alone, a 2-of-3 multisignature arrangement requires two keys to approve a transaction.

The transaction journey

One continuous transaction, start to finish.

  1. 01

    Agree on the terms

    Before money moves, both sides understand what is being purchased, what delivery means, and how the transaction will be completed.

    Terms set
  2. 02

    Secure the funds

    The buyer funds the escrow arrangement. A single participant cannot authorize the payout alone.

    Awaiting completion
  3. 03

    Complete the deal

    When the agreed conditions are met, the required signers authorize the payout. If the parties disagree, the defined dispute process determines the next step.

    Payout authorized

Built for both kinds of commerce

Digital or physical, the protection follows the terms.

Delivered online. Protected by agreement.

From software and digital downloads to design work and freelance services, the transaction can define what counts as delivery before funds are released.

Shipped in the real world. Protected by clear terms.

For physical products, agreed terms can account for shipping, tracking, receipt confirmation, and disputes. Delivery evidence must come from an actual verification process, not from the multisignature mechanism itself.

Trust the process, not just the promise.

Zecrow brings privacy-conscious payments and shared authorization into one transaction experience, giving buyers and sellers a clearer way to agree, transact, and resolve problems.
