Why online trade needs to change

Online commerce runs on trust. What happens when trust breaks?

Buyers risk paying for products that never arrive. Sellers risk delivering goods or completing work without getting paid. Traditional escrow can reduce that risk, but it often introduces another intermediary, extra fees, delays, and a new party both sides must trust.

  • For buyersPaying upfront without certainty that the seller will deliver.
  • For sellersHanding over goods or completing work before knowing whether payment is secure.
  • For digital goodsFiles, software, and creative work deliver instantly, but quality or compliance with requirements may be disputed.
  • For physical goodsShipping, tracking, receipt confirmation, and delivery disputes complicate the transaction.
  • For traditional escrowDepending on a central operator to control funds and decide how disagreements are resolved.

Zecrow’s proposed answer: shared cryptographic authorization instead of blind trust, structured evidence both sides can see, and AI-assisted review when stories conflict.

Introducing Zecrow

Your funds. Shared control. Evidence that matters.

Zecrow is designed to combine Zcash-based payments with a 2-of-3 multisignature escrow arrangement and AI-assisted evidence review. The goal is to reduce dependence on blind trust by making authorization rules, transaction terms, and dispute evidence central to the process.

Payment privacy and multisignature security are different properties. The proposed escrow uses transparent addresses, so its on-chain activity is visible — shielding payments and sharing authorization solve different problems.

The technology behind the trust

Rules enforced by cryptography, not promises.

Think of the escrow as a vault with three keyholes — buyer, seller, arbitrator. Any two keys together can authorize a payout, and no single key can move funds alone.

  1. 01

    Establish the transaction

    The buyer and seller agree on the amount, item or service, delivery requirements, and release conditions.

    Terms set
  2. 02

    Secure the funds

    The buyer funds the designated escrow arrangement. Spending requires two valid signatures from the three authorized participants.

    2 of 3 to authorize
  3. 03

    Authorize the outcome

    When the transaction is completed or resolved, the required signers authorize the relevant payout according to the agreed rules.

    Payout signed
  • No single controlOne participant cannot authorize a payout alone.
  • Limited arbitrator powerThe arbitrator can help resolve disputes without unrestricted unilateral control over funds.
  • AI stays separateThe authorization rules are enforced by signatures — independent of the AI review process.

Nothing here releases funds automatically, and there is no smart contract behind it — every payout follows the agreed rules through real signatures.

Intelligence for disputed transactions

When stories conflict, examine the evidence.

AI is intended to help organize and assess transaction evidence, identify inconsistencies, and prepare a useful review summary for the arbitrator — a major part of how Zecrow handles disagreement, not a side feature.

Collect→ Compare→ Identify→ Summarize
  • Assistant, not judgeAI helps review evidence; it is not the final decision-maker.
  • No invented proofAI cannot fabricate proof or treat an unverified claim as established fact.
  • No keysAI does not possess private keys and cannot independently authorize payouts.
  • Process decidesDisputed outcomes follow the defined authorization and arbitration process.

Built for the digital economy

Delivered online. Protected by agreed terms.

From software and freelance work to downloads and design files — delivery alone does not prove requirements were met. The original agreement and supporting evidence decide.

  • 01 AgreeBuyer and seller agree on the deliverable and acceptance criteria.
  • 02 FundThe escrow arrangement is funded.
  • 03 SubmitThe seller submits the agreed deliverable.
  • 04 Confirm or disputeThe buyer confirms completion, or a disagreement triggers evidence review.
  • 05 AI organizesThe available evidence is organized for the arbitrator.
  • 06 ResolveThe required signers authorize the resolution.

No automatic verification of file quality is claimed — acceptance follows the agreement and the evidence, not the delivery event.

Protection beyond the screen

Shipped in the real world. Governed by clear rules.

The seller prepares a package, the parcel ships, tracking evidence becomes available, and the buyer receives or disputes the delivery — with evidence reviewed when necessary.

  • Shipping & trackingRecords follow the parcel from seller to doorstep.
  • Delivery confirmationReceipt is confirmed against the agreed expectations.
  • Agreed deadlinesTerms set how long each stage may take.
  • Both sides heardEvidence can be submitted by buyer or seller.
  • Defined disputesA clear process decides disagreements — not whoever shouts loudest.

AI can organize tracking records and highlight inconsistencies, but it cannot independently prove a package arrived — reliable verification needs real evidence or a delivery integration.
