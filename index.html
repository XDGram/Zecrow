A clear process

Three steps. No guesswork.

The agreement is visible to both parties before money moves. Conditions are set up front, not improvised afterwards.

01 / FUND

Agree and fund

The seller creates an escrow with the amount, delivery terms and release condition. The buyer reviews and funds it.

02 / DELIVER

Complete the order

The seller delivers the goods or service and provides the agreed proof of delivery.

03 / RELEASE

Confirm or dispute

The buyer confirms the terms were met. If there is a problem, release pauses while the dispute is reviewed.

Privacy, without overpromising

Private payments.
Clear expectations.

Zcash shielded transactions can protect transaction details on-chain. Zecrow still needs to store the order terms, escrow status and evidence required to fulfil the agreement. Those are different layers of privacy.

Know what is protected

Payment privacy depends on the wallet and transaction path used. The app’s agreement records are not automatically hidden by Zcash. We’ll state these boundaries clearly before you fund an escrow.
